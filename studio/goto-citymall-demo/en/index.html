Welcome to Goto City Mall

A shopping mall on Fukue Island in Goto City, Nagasaki Prefecture. Supermarket, souvenirs, cafés and everyday shops under one roof.

Opening hours10:00–20:00Tsubakiya and DAISO open at 9:00. Hours vary by shop.
HolidaysOpen every day
ParkingFree, 450 cars

For travelers

  1. Souvenirs before your ferry or flight

    Nagasaki castella at Bunmeido Sohonten, Japanese sweets at Hatanaka, cakes and gifts at Bonpati.

  2. A quick meal or a break

    Café, pizza, udon noodles, takoyaki and a restaurant on the 1st floor.

  3. Everyday items

    Tsubakiya supermarket and the DAISO 100-yen shop, both on the 1st floor.

Signs in the mall are in Japanese. Show the shop name on this page to the staff if you need help.
