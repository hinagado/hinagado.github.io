Welcome to Goto City Mall

Opening hours10:00–20:00ParkingFree, 450 carsMost specialty shops close at 19:00. Tsubakiya and DAISO: 9:00–20:00

A shopping mall on Fukue Island in Goto City, Nagasaki Prefecture. Supermarket, souvenirs, cafés and everyday shops under one roof.

Days openOpen every day
Getting hereAbout 10 min by car from Fukue Port
About 15 min from Goto-Tsubaki AirportHow to get here
By local busGet off at the “City Mall-mae” (シティーモール前) stop. About 15–20 min from the Fukue bus stop; adult fare ¥330.Bus route in Google Maps(opens in a new tab)　Bus timetable (in Japanese)(opens in a new tab)
